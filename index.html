About Doorstep Design

At Doorstep Design, we believe that the holidays should be a time of joy, not stress. Founded in the heart of the Annapolis, Baltimore, and DC metropolitan area, we specialize in transforming homes into breathtaking seasonal showcases.

Our team of experienced designers and installers bring creativity, precision, and a passion for excellence to every project. Whether you're hosting an intimate gathering or creating a neighborhood spectacle, we work closely with you to bring your vision to life.

We pride ourselves on our white-glove service, premium materials, and meticulous attention to detail. From the first consultation to the final installation, we handle every aspect with care and professionalism.

Installation time varies depending on the scope of your project. A simple indoor tree and mantel setup might take 2-4 hours, while an elaborate full-home exterior and interior design can take 1-2 days. We'll provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.
